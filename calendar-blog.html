Dream - August 2021

I don't remember what forum I was on pre-2018 midterms. It was essentially 'whack-a-mole' during those years. We were kicked off 4Chan/pol, 8Chan/cbts, 8Chan/qresearch, and eventually landed at 8Kun. [Today is 8/7/2026; I can't remember off the top of my head which forum we were posting on. I may look it up later and update this post.] Regardless, we needed the 2018 midterms to avoid Trump becoming a lame duck and stalling or preventing the investigation into the Russia hoax, and other things. (Unfortunately, we were broadsided by Bill Barr.) My main mission was to get the base motivated to vote because historically, and even today, people don't vote during the midterms. It was the first time I heard of Charlie Kirk (someone had posted something—I can't remember if it was an event or what, but it was an image of him and something he was doing). I looked him up and saw his influence on the younger voting population and his ability to hold a captive audience with respect to government policies, which was very unusual. Most kids, especially when I was in college, had no idea about what was going on in the government, nor did we care about participating in a 'corrupt system.' I started posting his website and events on the forum, along with links to get people to volunteer. I watched some of his videos, but I didn't like the ones I saw because they were focused on "owning" someone. Instead of reasoning with them, it was more about how to make them look stupid or how to belittle and berate them. He was missing the opportunity to witness to those listening to the conversation. (I am only speaking of the very few videos I watched of him back then; maybe he wasn't always like that.) At that time, he didn't know who I was—until maybe the mid or end of 2019? I think it was 2019 when I attended a Women's Trump event in Las Vegas, and Charlie was one of the speakers, though I can't remember if I knew he was going to be there or not. I don't think I knew, because I was only aware of the event through Hibbs mentioning it to the congregation during a Sunday service. It was the first time I went to a political event, and I think it was the only time I heard Charlie speak live. [2018 and 2019 were the years I was evangelising. I had a full-time job that allowed me to drive around and talk to strangers.] I saw something that is so typical of Christians and why I generally avoided them. Originally, Charlie didn't speak much about Christianity, and so he was only being targeted by people who disagreed with his views, which were based on biblical doctrine. However, each year he became more vocal about his faith, he became more passionate with his audience and concerned about their future—not just physically. Charlie was transforming before our eyes, and the more vocal he was, the more Christians started to pick at him for not being 'spiritual enough.' They all had their own ideology of what that meant, and because he didn't fit their skewed, corrupted hearts, they claimed he wasn't a Christian and that they didn't agree with what he was doing, because: 'That's not what Christians should be doing,' 'It's too political,' and the worst one, 'Jesus never did that.' These were comments coming from reputable theologians, where biblical doctrine was, and still is, being taught. It was such a disgrace, but predictable. It's the main reason so many people are turned off from Christians or even learning about the Bible. Now, I have seen these things my whole life, so I wasn't shocked when it started happening, but my heart went out to Charlie because I don't think he was ready for it. Of all people, I think he thought he would have had more of a homecoming or at least some type of encouragement from that "brotherly love" community. So I started encouraging him, praying for him, and asking God to use him and empower him—that he would be bold and strengthened. And Charlie heard my prayer for him, believed it, and went out the next day and spoke to a church, edifying them through the power of the Holy Spirit. My heart broke for Charlie because he loved America so much. He really thought he could save it and was going to do everything possible to do so, but I knew something he didn't. I worried about how he would handle what was about to come to the United States: could he handle it, or would it break him? Every time I would watch one of his videos or speeches, I kept thinking about what would happen to him mentally and emotionally, and I would vocalize my concerns. It was encouraging that he didn't care; he was going to save America anyway—that was beautiful. Many people give up, move, pack their bags, and falsely believe they can't do anything about it; that wasn't Charlie. In August 2021, I had a dream. I had never dreamt of Charlie before and, as far as I remember now, I haven't dreamed of him since. But in that dream back in 2021, I woke up wondering if Charlie was going to heaven. It was as if it were a spring or summer day. It was sunny out, with no clouds and blue skies. He was walking up some concrete stairs and started walking down a concrete sidewalk path. On each side of the sidewalk, there was nice green grass, and not too far off in the distance was a building he was headed toward. It was a joyous occasion; it felt like there was happiness there, but with each step the weather changed. A snowstorm came, and he was in knee-deep snow. His demeanor changed, and he was very, very sad. There was so much feeling in that dream—I've never experienced that before. It was an agony of sadness. He didn't get far on that path before finally dropping to his knees. Then, it was as if he were dead; he just fell forward on his face. He didn't even try to brace himself—he just fell into the snow and didn't move, and I woke up saying,"Is Charlie going to _ _ _?"
